## **Summary**

The frontier AI industry is fundamentally transforming into an energy and physical infrastructure business where model scaling is directly bottlenecked by power generation and grid coordination. While China aggressively expands 500+ GW of centralized energy capacity over 5 years, Western progress relies on unlocking over 100 GW of stranded US power through modular grid pods, custom specialized silicon, and decentralized Reinforcement Learning orchestration. Financial markets are undergoing a severe reckoning as traditional Venture Capital marks in legacy software erode, shifting institutional capital toward deep technology, sovereign enterprise compute, and dynamic compute pooling networks like **National Compute** to maintain Western technical leadership.

## **The Core Bottleneck and Material Science Breakthroughs**

The core business of frontier AI companies like **Anthropic** involves purchasing megawatts of energy and converting them into tokens of intelligence. While the marginal cost of creating software has dropped to zero, progress in frontier model intelligence is bounded by physical energy production in the US and the West.

Long term energy transmission constraints have prompted focused research into room temperature superconductors. **AMP** backed **Periodic Labs** to accelerate the Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) loop between physical laboratories and AI research. **Periodic Labs** has discovered 13 new materials, including one currently utilized for ballistic heat shields. However, because the timeline for a room temperature superconductor breakthrough remains unpredictable, scaling frontier infrastructure requires executing immediate plan B and plan C operational strategies alongside fundamental material science research.

## **Energy Disparities and Decentralized RL Compute**

China is building over 500+ GW of industrial energy capacity over 5 years dedicated to AI capability scaling by prioritizing industrial capacity over civil liberties. In contrast, the US struggles with infrastructure scaling and coordination, leaving over 100 GW of stranded power in decommissioned factories, military land, and isolated sites that are not connected to the electrical grid.

To address this challenge without reliance on gigawatt scale centralized builds, Western developers are leveraging Reinforcement Learning (RL) and post-training, which account for approximately 50% of compute spend across portfolio companies. RL workloads operate effectively on decentralized compute networks.

**Mavria**, co-founded in March by former **Department of Energy** Engineers, manufactures 5 to 10 MW atomic prefabricated grid pods in Houston. Plugging these pods directly into stranded power pockets lowers baseline electricity costs for local ratepayer communities, while instantly supplying compute to researchers.

## **National Compute Grid and the Goodput Model**

In March, **AMP** co-founded **National Compute** to establish the National Compute Grid, an interconnected network linking real estate, power generation, and distributed compute facilities. Since January, powered land attached to these clusters has appreciated by multiple factors.

| Compute Category | Pricing / Allocation Model | Operational Impact |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Bleeding Edge Spot Compute (**AMD** MI355X) | \$12 per hour on-demand clearing price | Replaces 5 year leases (\$3 to \$4 per hour baseline) with flexible usage. |
| Total Compute Spend | Pay per completed work (Goodput metric) | Reduces effective lab spend by 30% to 40% by eliminating idle capacity charges. |
| Infrastructure Scheduling | AI agent (Marshall) via **Google** Borg architecture | Maximizes GPU utilization across the West from 60% baseline to 99%. |
| Grid Infrastructure Capacity (2030) | 2 GW anchored by **Zurich**; 5 GW third-party | 25% target allocation reserved for custom specialized silicon. |

The grid relies on an AI scheduling agent named Marshall, built on the internal **Google** Borg orchestration system developed by **National Compute** founder [Sebastian Lobo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sebastianlobo/), which previously raised GPU utilization across 1,000,000+ accelerators at **Google** from \~60% to 99%. Marshall decouples physical clusters from atomic units of work, pricing compute dynamically based on completed work, or Goodput.

Framing AI spend strictly in terms of training versus inference represents an imprecise infrastructure view rather than a scientific one. Frontier scientists manage pipelines divided into pre-training, mid-training, post-training, and deployment, where post-training involves extensive inference fed back into model weights.

## **Venture Capital Realignment and Deep Tech Exits**

Over the past decade, Venture Capital expanded into an 800 billion asset class, but significant portions were managed by growth equity practitioners operating as glorified bankers using spreadsheets without technical or scientific backgrounds. Many funds deployed sovereign and pension capital into payroll software and fintech at peak 2021 entry valuations, creating non-viable marks that face imminent writedowns.

Institutional limited partners are actively rotating capital back to early-stage deep technology, mirroring early venture models such as **Kleiner Perkins** partnering on day one with hard technology companies like **Genentech**, **Intel**, and **Apple**.

Milestone driven deal structuring yields substantial venture returns when tied directly to operational performance metrics like platform token flow. For example, **OpenRouter** was acquired by **Stripe** for north of 7 billion. **a16z** led the Series A round with an approximate 20% ownership stake, returning over 1 billion to limited partners within 8 months.

## **Specialized Silicon and Sovereign Enterprise Compute**

Custom silicon development is accelerating as architectural stability settles around specific model checkpoints and Mixture of Experts (MoE) designs. Custom silicon taped for standardized RL encoding or specific model checkpoints can deliver 1,000x efficiency gains over general purpose GPUs, justifying valuations for specialized chip designers like **Fractile**, which raised at 40 billion.

Specialized chips enable ultrafast token throughput, eliminating developer wait times when monitoring agents via dual screen mobile hardware like the iPhone Duo.

For enterprise adoption, sovereign compute running on-premises is replacing public cloud dependencies due to strict regulatory limits:

* US Cloud Act regulations grant the US government access to AI workloads hosted on domestic public infrastructure like **AWS** or **GCP**.  
* **ASML** invested 2 billion directly into **Mistral** to secure sovereign European compute capabilities independent of US cloud providers.  
* Enterprise deployment models are shifting toward open weight architectures, allowing regulated industries in Healthcare, Defense, and Finance to own their reasoning loops locally.  
* Sovereign open model infrastructure providers, including **Mistral** (which scaled from a 2 billion to 25 billion valuation), **Black Forest Labs**, and **River AI**, operate similarly to **Red Hat** in enterprise Linux environments.

## **Compute Pooling, Neoclouds, and Market Defensibility**

Maintaining Western technical superiority requires aggressive hardware pooling across diverse silicon suppliers, including **Nvidia** and **AMD**, to eliminate structural redundancy. Fragmented Western efforts currently lag behind coordinated Chinese state allocations.

A 24,000 GPU cluster inside the US was presold to one of China's four largest tech companies because Neocloud providers lacked sufficient Western credit backing and relied on upfront cash from state supported Chinese entities. To counter this structural exposure, **AMP** partnered with **Zurich** to establish a multibillion dollar rescue fund that buys out and reallocates Chinese contracted clusters back to Western development teams.

Neocloud entities operate fundamentally as capital intensive operations businesses rather than high margin software platforms, leaving them exposed to severe nation-state cybersecurity risks.

Standalone inference providers lack long term business defensibility. Because inference is a feature within the feedback loop rather than a standalone product, frontier research teams at **Anthropic** and **Mistral** are actively bringing inference pipelines in-house. Similarly, manual data annotation sweatshops face commoditization, whereas continuous model evaluation platforms like **Arena** have scaled past 200 million in annualized consumption by embedding directly into automated lab workflows.
