# **Startup TNT Investment Summit XI 2025**

## **Executive Summary**

This document synthesizes the key outcomes, investment data, and company profiles from the **Startup TNT** Investment Summit XI held in Calgary. The document covers both the Top 20 Pitch Night (April 3, 2025\) and the Finale Pitch Night (May 1, 2025), detailing the growth of the regional innovation ecosystem and the specific startups receiving investment.

**Startup TNT**’s Investment Summit XI marks a significant milestone in the Western Canadian venture capital landscape, coinciding with the organization's five-year anniversary. Since its inception in 2019, **Startup TNT** has facilitated over $17.4 million in investments across 110 companies, supported by a network of nearly 490 angel investors.

The Calgary chapter has been particularly impactful, activating 157 local angel investors and deploying $7.4 million into 40 companies. Summit XI concluded with **ResVR** being named the primary winner, while three additional side deals were secured for **Pharma Pilot**, **Village Wellth**, and **Tarkka Manufacturing Solutions**. The summit highlighted a diverse array of sectors, including medical devices, AI-driven real estate, quantum computing, and clean technology.

## **Organizational Overview: The Startup TNT Model**

**Startup TNT** (originally "Thursday Night Tradition") began as a grassroots initiative in Edmonton to address the shortage of early-stage capital. It has since expanded across Western Canada, including Calgary, Saskatchewan, Winnipeg, and British Columbia.

### **Investment Process**

* **Syndicated Angel Investing:** Investors pre-commit a minimum of $5,000 each before knowing the eventual recipient. For Summit XI, a pool of approximately 34-42 investors participated.  
* **Team-Based Due Diligence:** The process is not a simple pitch competition but a six-week collaborative due diligence period. Investors review data rooms, interview founders, examine financials, and build relationships.  
* **Active Investing:** While the summit crowns a single winner for the main syndicate, the model frequently results in side deals where individual investors or sub-groups fund other finalists.  
* **Market Position:** **Startup TNT** is currently ranked as the most active pre-seed investor in Canada and the second-highest private non-government agency for seed-stage deals.

### **Regional Impact (Calgary)**

The Calgary ecosystem demonstrates high levels of diversity and engagement:

* **Total Investment:** $7.4 million (43% of **Startup TNT**'s total capital).  
* **Founder Diversity:** 53% of companies are female-founded and 25% are BIPOC founded.  
* **Institutional Support:** Strong integration with **Platform Calgary**, the **University of Calgary** (Hunter Hub), and **Alberta Innovates**.

# **Startup TNT Investment Summit XII 2026**

## **Executive Summary**

The Calgary Investment Summit Spring 2026, hosted by **Startup TNT**, represents the culmination of a six week diligence process involving 52 startup applications. This cycle engaged a group of 185 angel investors in Calgary who each contributed a minimum of 5,000 dollars to a pre-committed capital pool for the winner. Historical data indicates that **Startup TNT** has facilitated 21 million dollars of capital investment into 128 companies over the past seven years, with 13 million dollars specifically allocated to 39 Calgary, based companies.

The Spring 2026 summit featured five finalists representing diverse sectors including medical technology, residential services, legal technology, and renewable energy. **Robinson Exotech** was announced as the winner of the summit for its development of the XLEG, a wearable, hands free crutch alternative. Other finalists, including **CliniQuill**, **Home Spritz**, **Jora AI**, and **Solartility**, presented significant traction, with several companies reporting annual recurring revenue between 330,000 and 2 million dollars. The event also highlighted the role of ecosystem partners such as **Osler**, **MNP**, **Alberta Innovates**, the **Hunter Hub**, and **Platform Calgary** in supporting local innovation and capital allocation.

## **The Startup TNT Investment Process**

The summit serves as the final step in a structured evaluation and diligence framework designed to activate local angel investors.

* **Diligence Framework:** Investors spent several weeks reviewing 52 applications, meeting directly with founders, and evaluating data rooms.  
* **Capital Allocation:** The investment pool is formed by individual contributions of 5,000 dollars from each participating angel investor.  
* **Community Impact:** The process aims to engage the local community to support high, growth startups. "The 21 million of capital invested into 128 companies and 550 angel investors engaged over the past seven years could not be possible without the community and people who show up."

## **Analysis of Finalist Companies**

### **Robinson Exotech (Summit Winner)**

**Robinson Exotech** developed the XLEG, a mobility device designed for users recovering from lower limb injuries. The product allows for hands, free movement and normal walking patterns while remaining non weight bearing.

* **Product Development:** The founder developed the first prototype following a severe car crash involving 13 surgeries. The current version, version five, is under seven pounds, with version six and seven in development to further reduce weight and transition to an off the shelf product.  
* **Regulatory and Market Status:** The device is registered with the FDA as a class one medical device. It is patented in Canada, the United States, Australia, and China, with pending patents in 30 European countries.  
* **Financial Metrics:** The company maintains zero debt. Projected margins scale from 19 percent to 56 percent as revenue reaches a target of 10 million dollars.  
* **Social Impact:** The device aims to reduce muscle atrophy and blood clots while allowing injured individuals to return to work sooner. "Having that storefront is going to bring in business to begin with, nationally we've got a million people here in Canada and then of course, on the south of the border we've got six million."

### **CliniQuill**

**Clinquil** provides AI powered clinical documentation tools specifically designed for community based nurses and allied health professionals.

* **Problem Statement:** Community health providers often spend 40 to 50 percent of their time on paperwork, often in environments without internet access.  
* **Technical Differentiation:** Unlike standard AI scribes, **CliniQuill** is built for rural environments without internet, supports image based documentation for wound care, and accommodates non linear workflows.  
* **Validation:** The company received funding from Canada Health Infoway to build and evaluate the tool, despite being off cycle for the national program.  
* **Commercial Status:** **Clinquil** launched commercially in May 2026\. The CEO recently stepped down from a leadership role at **OKAKI** to lead the company full time.

### **Home Spritz**

**Home Spritz** operates a residential cleaning platform that emphasizes cleaner retention and service quality to address industry turnover.

* **Operational Model:** The company acts as a certified living wage employer in Alberta. It uses AI to automate quoting, scheduling, and cleaner matching.  
* **Performance Metrics:** Cleaner turnover is approximately 20 percent, compared to an industry average of 300 percent. Repeat client rates exceed 85 percent.  
* **Financial Growth:** The company is on track for 2 million dollars in revenue for 2026\. It takes a 33 percent fee from every cleaning.  
* **Expansion Strategy:** Currently operating in Alberta, British Columbia, and Ontario. The long term vision includes white labeling the platform infrastructure for other service industries. "Once we really prove our scalability in cleaning business we want to expand this to, into other service industries through white labeling and also marketplace partnerships."

### **Jora AI**

**Jora** provides an AI, powered platform designed to automate real estate conveyancing for law firms.

* **Market Opportunity:** Real estate law involves highly repetitive, transaction based work where efficiency is critical to margins. **Jora** targets the British Commonwealth market, which has an estimated total addressable market of 1 billion dollars.  
* **Revenue Traction:** The company currently has 330,000 dollars in annualized recurring revenue from two firms. Signed contracts starting in October 2026 are projected to increase annualized revenue to 2.5 million dollars by year end.  
* **Product Benefits:** The platform automates document ingestion, data validation, and issue identification. It can increase the capacity of a solo lawyer to handle up to 30 closings a month, representing a high enterprise value.  
* **Funding Goal:** **Jora** is raising 1.5 million dollars to scale sales operations and expand product offerings.

### **Solartility**

**Solartility** is an electric retailer building Alberta's first retail virtual power plant by aggregating distributed energy resources like home solar panels and batteries.

* **Grid Solution:** The company addresses grid instability and rising demand by making residential solar systems grid aware. This allows thousands of home batteries to supply the grid during peak demand without building new power plants.  
* **Unit Economics:** **Solartility** generates 50 dollars per month per customer, significantly higher than the 5 to 10 dollar margin typical of traditional retailers. This is achieved by avoiding affiliate sales commissions and hedging costs through battery management.  
* **Scaling Strategy:** The company is establishing a 40 million dollar independent fund to cover equipment and installation costs for 1,000 homes. Homeowners pay for electricity at lower rates, while the fund generates returns and **Solartility** collects grid service revenue.  
* **Current Traction:** The company has 1,050 paying customers and 4 million dollars in committed non-dilutive funding. "In a single phrase, the operating system of the future grid."
